Simple strength routine

Start with 5 minutes of easy warm-up. On strength days, try 1–2 comfortable rounds of:

Chair squats — 8–12 repetitions
Wall or incline push-ups — 6–10 repetitions
Glute bridges — 8–12 repetitions
Bird-dogs — 6–8 per side
Easy cool-down — 3–5 minutes

Use controlled movements and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms. Adjust exercises to your needs; seek professional advice for persistent neck/back symptoms.
